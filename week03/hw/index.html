Strawberry Tiramisu

About

Strawberry Tiramisu is a sweet fruity twist on the classic Italian dessert, made with fresh strawberries, coffee-soaked ladyfingers, and creamy mascarpone.

It is best chilled overnight before serving.

Ingredients

Coffee Soak

  • 1 1/4 cups espresso
  • 6 tablespoons sweet Marsala wine
  • 1 tablespoon vanilla extract

Strawberry Filling

  • 600g strawberries
  • 100g sugar
  • Pinch of salt

Mascarpone Filling

  • 2 cups mascarpone cheese
  • 1/4 cup sweet Marsala wine
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 5 large eggs
  • 100g sugar
  • 2 cups whipping cream

Other

  • 300g ladyfinger cookies
  • Cocoa powder

Instructions

  1. Make the coffee soak and let it cool.
  2. Prepare the strawberries by cutting them into thin slices and mixing them with sugar and a pinch of salt. Let them sit for 30 minutes.
  3. Mix the mascarpone, Marsala wine, and vanilla together.
  4. Prepare the eggs and add them to the mascarpone filling.
  5. Whip the cream and gently fold it into the mascarpone mixture.
  6. Quickly dip the ladyfingers into the coffee mixture.
  7. Place the ladyfingers in a single layer in a pan.
  8. Add a layer of strawberries.
  9. Add a layer of mascarpone cream.
  10. Repeat the layers until the pan is filled.
  11. Cover and refrigerate overnight.
  12. Dust with cocoa powder before serving.

Tips

  • Do not soak the ladyfingers for too long.
  • Let the tiramisu chill overnight.
  • Add the cocoa powder right before serving and if you want starwberries!

Enjoy!
